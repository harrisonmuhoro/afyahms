# Post-Signature SOAP Note Actions — Afya HMS

## The Core Rule

> **Once signed, the note is locked.** No edit button should ever appear for the signing doctor. That is a legal and audit requirement. Signing = finalization.

---

## Buttons by Role & Context

### 👨‍⚕️ Signing Doctor (post-sign view)

| Button | Why |
|---|---|
| **Print / Export PDF** | Physical or referral copy |
| **Add Addendum** | Correct without altering the original — preserves audit trail |
| **Order Labs** | Follow through on plan immediately |
| **Refer Patient** | Trigger referral workflow from the plan |
| **Schedule Follow-Up** | Book next appointment directly |
| **Send to Pharmacy** | Push prescriptions from the plan to dispensary queue |

> **Primary CTA:** `Send to Pharmacy` + `Schedule Follow-Up` — the two most immediate next steps after signing.

---

### 🧑‍⚕️ Nurse (viewing signed note)

| Button | Why |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge** | Confirms they have seen care instructions |
| **Record Vitals** | If follow-up vitals were ordered |
| **Administer Medication** | Mark drugs as given |

---

### 🏥 Admin / Reception

| Button | Why |
|---|---|
| **Generate Invoice** | Bill based on the visit and plan |
| **Book Follow-Up** | Schedule from the plan's follow-up section |
| **Print Summary** | Patient-facing discharge summary |

---

### 👤 Patient (patient portal view)

| Button | Why |
|---|---|
| **Download My Summary** | Plain-language version of the visit |
| **Book Follow-Up** | Self-schedule if system supports it |

---

## Recommended Primary Flow

```
Doctor clicks [Sign & Lock]
        ↓
Note is timestamped + locked
        ↓
Modal or action bar appears:

  [📋 Add Addendum]   [💊 Send to Pharmacy]   [📅 Schedule Follow-Up]
  [🔬 Order Labs]     [↗ Refer Patient]        [🖨 Print / Export PDF]
```

> The **modal approach** (pop up after signing) is better than hiding buttons in the note header.
> It forces the doctor to consciously choose next steps, reducing dropped follow-ups.

---

## What You Should NOT Do ❌

- ❌ Show an **Edit** button — breaks the legal record
- ❌ Show **Delete** — ever, for anyone, under any role
- ❌ Show **all buttons to all roles** — role-based visibility is critical (handled by Spatie RBAC)
- ❌ Auto-redirect away from the note immediately after signing — doctor may need to review what they just signed

---

## Afya HMS Implementation — Spatie Permission Gates

Gate each post-signature button by its corresponding Spatie permission:

```php
'soap_notes.export_pdf'
'soap_notes.add_addendum'
'pharmacy.receive_prescription'
'appointments.create'
'billing.generate_invoice'
'lab_orders.create'
'referrals.create'
```

Each button renders **only** if the authenticated user holds the matching permission:

```php
// Blade example
@can('soap_notes.add_addendum')
    <button>Add Addendum</button>
@endcan

@can('pharmacy.receive_prescription')
    <button>Send to Pharmacy</button>
@endcan

@can('billing.generate_invoice')
    <button>Generate Invoice</button>
@endcan
```

## Audit Trail Requirements

Every post-signature action must be logged:

```
[2026-08-06 14:35 EAT] SOAP Note #4821 — Signed by Dr. A. Njoroge
[2026-08-06 14:36 EAT] SOAP Note #4821 — Prescription sent to Pharmacy by Dr. A. Njoroge
[2026-08-06 14:37 EAT] SOAP Note #4821 — Follow-up scheduled (20/08/2026) by Dr. A. Njoroge
[2026-08-06 14:50 EAT] SOAP Note #4821 — Acknowledged by Nurse J. Waweru
[2026-08-06 15:02 EAT] SOAP Note #4821 — Invoice #INV-2026-0093 generated by Admin C. Muthoni
```

Log every action with: `actor`, `role`, `action`, `timestamp`, `note_id`.

---

## Bottom Line

> The **primary button** after signing is contextually **"Send to Pharmacy"** + **"Schedule Follow-Up"** for the doctor.
> Supporting actions are available to the full care team, all gated by Spatie role-permission.
> **No one ever edits or deletes a signed note — only addendums are permitted.**
