# 🧑‍⚕️ Role: Patient (Patient Portal / Self-Service)
> **Scope:** Appointment booking, personal health record access, prescription history, lab result viewing, bill payment, and communication with care team.  
> **Accountability:** Personal. You are responsible for the accuracy of information you provide and the security of your own account. Providing false information or misusing the system harms you and others.

---

## ✅ DO's

### Registration & Identity
- **DO** provide accurate, complete, and truthful personal information at registration — your name, date of birth, ID number, phone, allergies, and current medications.
- **DO** update your contact details in the system whenever they change — appointment reminders and critical lab notifications depend on correct contact info.
- **DO** carry a valid identity document to every visit — it is used to verify your identity before any clinical interaction.
- **DO** inform the receptionist or nurse of any allergies, chronic conditions, or current medications at every visit — even if you believe it's on file.

### Appointments & Queue
- **DO** book appointments in advance where possible — walk-in queues are longer and less predictable.
- **DO** arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to complete check-in.
- **DO** cancel or reschedule appointments you cannot attend at least 24 hours in advance — your slot can be given to another patient who needs it.
- **DO** inform the front desk immediately if your condition worsens while you are waiting — do not silently suffer in the queue.
- **DO** follow queue instructions from the receptionist and nursing staff — the order exists for clinical safety reasons, not convenience.

### Health Information & Portal Access
- **DO** review your lab results, prescriptions, and visit summaries through the patient portal when available.
- **DO** ask the doctor or nurse to explain any result or prescription you don't understand — you have a right to understand your own care.
- **DO** keep your portal login credentials private — your health records contain sensitive personal information.
- **DO** report any portal discrepancy (wrong result, someone else's data, missing record) to the medical records office immediately.

### Medications & Follow-Up
- **DO** take prescribed medications exactly as instructed — dose, frequency, route, and duration.
- **DO** complete the full course of antibiotics or any prescribed treatment course, even if you feel better early.
- **DO** return for scheduled follow-up appointments — many conditions worsen silently without the patient noticing.
- **DO** bring all current medications (or a list of them) to every visit — including supplements, herbal remedies, and OTC drugs.
- **DO** report adverse drug reactions or side effects to your doctor immediately — do not stop medication without consulting the clinician first.

### Payments
- **DO** request and keep all payment receipts — they are your proof of transaction in case of any dispute.
- **DO** review your bill before payment and ask for clarification on any line item you don't recognize.
- **DO** use the facility's official M-Pesa paybill or payment channels — never pay directly to an individual.

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

### Information & Identity
- **DON'T** provide false or exaggerated information to obtain medications, referrals, or insurance benefits — this is fraud and can harm your clinical care.
- **DON'T** register under a different name or ID to avoid a record of a visit — your clinical history protects you.
- **DON'T** allow someone else to use your patient account or attend a visit on your behalf without official proxy authorization.

### Clinical Safety
- **DON'T** self-diagnose and request specific tests or medications without going through the clinical consultation process — the doctor's assessment is not optional.
- **DON'T** conceal relevant medical history (drug use, prior surgeries, sexual history, mental health) — what you hide can harm you. The clinical team is bound by confidentiality.
- **DON'T** stop or alter your prescribed medication without consulting the clinician — even if you're feeling better or experiencing mild side effects.
- **DON'T** share your prescription medications with anyone else — what is safe for you may be dangerous for another person.
- **DON'T** use a prescription from a previous visit to obtain medication without a new consultation if your condition has changed.

### Portal & Privacy
- **DON'T** share your patient portal login with family or friends — if they need access, request an authorized proxy through the records office.
- **DON'T** post your medical records, lab results, or prescription details on social media or share via WhatsApp — once shared, you cannot control who sees them.
- **DON'T** attempt to modify your own records or results in the system — this is a criminal act and will result in account suspension.

### Conduct
- **DON'T** be abusive or threatening toward any hospital staff — it will result in your removal from the premises and potential blacklisting.
- **DON'T** demand to bypass the queue — emergency prioritization is determined clinically by nursing triage, not by patient request.
- **DON'T** record clinical consultations without the explicit consent of the attending clinician — it is illegal in many contexts and violates the privacy of other patients.

---

## ⚠️ Critical Reminders
- **You are the most important person in your care team** — the system works best when you are honest, informed, and engaged.
- Your health history is yours. Protect access to it, but don't hide it from the people treating you.
- Missed follow-ups are the most common reason manageable conditions become emergencies. Show up.
