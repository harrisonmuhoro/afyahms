# 💰 Role: Cashier (Billing & Payments)
> **Scope:** Invoice generation, payment collection, insurance claim processing, receipt issuance, and financial reconciliation.  
> **Accountability:** Financial integrity. Every shilling must be accounted for. Errors here are either theft, fraud, or negligence — and all three have consequences.

---

## ✅ DO's

### Billing & Invoicing
- **DO** generate invoices only from completed, system-confirmed service records — never bill for services not documented by the clinical team.
- **DO** verify that all clinical services rendered in a visit are captured on the invoice before presenting it to the patient — missed billing is revenue loss.
- **DO** apply the correct pricing tier based on the patient's insurance scheme, SHA category, or self-pay status as registered in the system.
- **DO** itemize every invoice clearly — patients and insurers have the right to know exactly what they're paying for.
- **DO** flag any discrepancy between what was ordered and what appears on the bill before finalizing — resolve it through the clinical team, not by guessing.

### Payment Processing
- **DO** issue an official system-generated receipt for every payment received — no manual receipts, no "I'll sort it later."
- **DO** confirm the payment mode and amount before completing any transaction: cash, M-Pesa, card, or insurance.
- **DO** verify M-Pesa transaction confirmation codes against the system before marking a payment as received.
- **DO** balance your cash drawer at the start and end of every shift — document and escalate any discrepancy immediately.
- **DO** process refunds only through the system's official refund workflow with supervisor approval — never from your cash drawer unilaterally.

### Insurance & SHA Claims
- **DO** verify insurance eligibility in the system before applying scheme pricing — unverified insurance that gets rejected leaves the hospital with a loss.
- **DO** submit insurance claims within the insurer's defined submission window — late claims are routinely rejected.
- **DO** document all claim submission details: scheme, claim number, amount, date, and status.
- **DO** follow up on pending or rejected claims proactively — an unresolved rejected claim is lost revenue.
- **DO** maintain a register of all waived, discounted, or partially paid bills with supervisor authorization documentation.

### Financial Controls
- **DO** escalate any request to waive, discount, or write off a bill to the appropriate supervisor — this is never your unilateral decision.
- **DO** report any unusual payment patterns or pressure from staff/patients to manipulate bills to the finance manager immediately.

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

### Financial Integrity
- **DON'T** collect payment without issuing a system receipt — cash collected without a receipt is untracked and exposes you to fraud allegations.
- **DON'T** process a payment for a visit that is clinically incomplete — the doctor must have closed the consultation before billing is finalized.
- **DON'T** split bills, alter invoice amounts, or apply unauthorized discounts — every adjustment needs a documented authorization trail.
- **DON'T** accept payment on behalf of another cashier or station — each transaction must be tied to the logged-in cashier.
- **DON'T** keep undeposited cash in your drawer overnight — follow the facility's cash handling and banking schedule.

### Scope Violations
- **DON'T** access or modify clinical records (diagnoses, prescriptions, nursing notes) — your view is billing-relevant service records only.
- **DON'T** reverse or void an invoice without supervisor approval and a documented reason.
- **DON'T** make insurance eligibility decisions — use the system and escalate edge cases to the billing supervisor.
- **DON'T** release a patient's billing history to third parties without the appropriate authorization.

### Privacy & Ethics
- **DON'T** discuss a patient's bill amount or payment status in public areas — financial information is as sensitive as clinical information.
- **DON'T** share your login credentials — every transaction is tied to your account and you bear personal accountability for it.
- **DON'T** allow another staff member to process payments from your logged-in terminal.

### Errors & Disputes
- **DON'T** resolve a billing dispute by silently adjusting figures — escalate to supervisor, document the dispute, and apply corrections through the proper workflow.
- **DON'T** ignore a patient complaint about an incorrect bill — it is both a financial and a reputational issue.

---

## ⚠️ Critical Reminders
- **An unreceipted payment is an unaccountable payment** — it will be attributed to you, not to the void.
- Insurance rejections due to incomplete or incorrect claims are preventable losses. Own the accuracy of what you submit.
- Pressure to manipulate a bill — from any source — is a red flag. Escalate it. Silence makes you complicit.
